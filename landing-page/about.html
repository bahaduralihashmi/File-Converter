👋 About All Files Converter AI

Our Mission

To make file conversion accessible, secure, and completely free for everyone. We believe that software should empower users, not limit them.

Who We Are

We are the generatewithali team - a group of passionate developers, designers, and testers from Riphah International University, Lahore. We met through our shared love for technology and solving real-world problems.

Why We Built This

We noticed a gap in the market:

  • ❌ Most file converters are expensive with subscription fees
  • ❌ Online converters are insecure – your files go to unknown servers
  • ❌ Free tools often have limited format support
  • ❌ Many apps are difficult to use for beginners

So we built All Files Converter AI – a free, offline, secure, and powerful converter that supports 1000+ formats with AI assistance, voice commands, and batch processing.
