Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how All Files Converter AI and allfileconvertor.site handle information. We aim to keep supported desktop conversion workflows local while being transparent about third-party technologies used by the website.

2. Desktop application

All Files Converter AI is designed as a Windows desktop application. Its supported file-conversion workflows are intended to process files locally on the user's computer. The website does not provide an online file-upload conversion endpoint, and files selected for local conversion are not intentionally sent to this website.

3. Website information and third-party services

The website may use third-party services for advertising, fonts, icons and related functionality. Those providers may process technical information under their own policies. We do not sell personal information.

  • Google AdSense: may be used for website advertising.
  • Google Fonts: used for website typography.
  • Other embedded services: may process requests according to their own policies when their resources are loaded.

4. Google advertising

If advertising is served, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Advertising technologies can involve cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and other technical information.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services for Google's explanation of its practices.

5. Advertising choices

Users can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Google's general privacy information is available through its Privacy Policy. Users can also review broader advertising choices at YourAdChoices.

6. Cookies and browser controls

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for website preferences and advertising when those services are active. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies can affect some website features and advertising functionality.

7. Data security and file privacy

Because supported desktop conversion workflows are designed for local processing, files selected for conversion are not intentionally uploaded to this website. Users should still keep their operating system, browser and desktop application environment secure and use software from trusted sources.

8. Children's privacy

The website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the website, application or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
