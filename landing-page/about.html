About All Files Converter AI

All Files Converter AI is a Windows desktop project focused on making everyday file conversion easier to understand and more practical. Supported conversion workflows are designed to run locally on the user's computer.

Our mission

Our goal is to bring useful document, image, audio, video, archive, e-book and spreadsheet workflows together in one desktop application while providing clear information about what the software does.

Who we are

We are the generatewithali team, a group of developers, designers, testers and documentation contributors from Riphah International University, Lahore. The project grew from our interest in software development and solving practical file-management problems.

Why we built this

We wanted a desktop converter that could bring common workflows together instead of requiring users to switch between many separate tools. We also wanted to explore batch processing, voice commands and AI-assisted workflows.

Current project scope

  • Document and text conversion workflows
  • Image conversion
  • Audio and video conversion
  • Archive and compressed-file workflows
  • E-book and spreadsheet workflows
  • Batch processing for supported conversions
  • AI-assisted and voice-oriented features in the desktop project
